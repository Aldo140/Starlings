# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Young people affected by parental or familial substance use, often visiting on a phone and looking for anonymous, approachable support, shared experiences, and trustworthy resources.

## Product Purpose

Starlings Support Map helps young people find peer-led and evidence-informed support, explore community resources, ask questions, and share notes without revealing their identity. Success means visitors can quickly feel less alone and reach relevant help without friction.

## Brand Personality

Warm, hopeful, and youth-centered. The experience should feel human and encouraging while remaining calm enough for sensitive reading.

## Anti-references

Avoid clinical or institutional presentation, generic template aesthetics, childish decoration, and ambient effects that distract from reading or slow the mobile experience.

## Design Principles

- Make safety, anonymity, and quick exit behavior immediately understandable.
- Keep important reading calm and legible while reserving playfulness for supportive moments.
- Use Starlings identity and community imagery with purpose, not as visual noise.
- Design mobile as a primary experience rather than a compressed desktop layout.
- Keep pathways to resources and contribution clear, direct, and welcoming.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain strong text contrast, clear focus states, keyboard navigation, comfortable touch targets, reduced-motion support, and performant mobile behavior. Decorative motion must never block interaction or be required to understand content.
